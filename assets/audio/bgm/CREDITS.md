# Background music
Downloaded 2026-10-16. Both tracks are offered under CC0 by the named authors.

Menu: “SpacyLoop” — ElectronicFreaker
https://opengameart.org/content/spacyloop
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Flight: “8-bit Epic Space Shooter Music” (8bit-spaceshooter.mp3) — HydroGene
https://opengameart.org/content/8-bit-epic-space-shooter-music
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Original files retained here. Game copies use FFmpeg loudness normalization
(I=-20LUFS, TP=-3dB, LRA=8),32kHz stereo,96kbps MP3. Playback uses a low music gain
and momentary ducking under important effects. Menu and flight loop separately.
